# Granular Access Control Errors

Granular Access Control (GAC) errors can help you manage and use applications effectively. This page shows how to troubleshoot some of the most common GAC errors you might encounter. 

## 404 - page not found

You have access to the application. However, after a successful login, you receive a 404 - Page Not Found error when trying to access it.

#### Cause

The 404 - Page Not Found error occurs when you do not have access to the requested page. In this case, access to the Home page is not provided, resulting in the error.

#### Solution

You must contact your instance administrator and request that they provide you with access to the Home page.

## Make application public toggle is not available

The _Make Application Public_ toggle in the invite users modal is disabled, preventing you from making an application public.

#### Cause

The _Make Application Public_ toggle is controlled by the _Make Public_ permission. If you do not have this permission, the toggle is disabled. For more information, see [Make Public Permission](/advanced-concepts/granular-access-control/reference/permissions#make-public-permission)

#### Solution


* You must contact your instance administrator and request that they provide you with the appropriate access..
* Alternatively, reach out to users who have the necessary permissions to make an application public.

## Sharing application shares entire workspace

When you share an application using the Share button, the entire workspace is shared with the user instead of the application.

#### Cause

* Your instance may not be activated with your license key, causing this paid feature to be unavailable.
* If you are using the Community Edition or a Free plan on the Appsmith Commercial Edition, sharing an application is not available. Consequently, sharing an application results in the entire workspace being shared.

#### Solution

* Activate your instance by adding your license key. For more details, see [Add a license key to your instance](/getting-started/setup/manage-plans/upgrade-plan?current-license-mode=add-license-key).
* If you are on a Community Edition or Free plan and need this feature, consider reviewing the [Business plan](https://www.appsmith.com/pricing) offerings and sign up on [customer.appsmith.com](https://customer.appsmith.com) to generate a license key.

## Unable to access application

A custom role that provides access to the application is created, but users are still unable to access the application.

#### Cause

* The custom role may not be assigned to the user trying to access the application. 
* The access to the application's datasources and environments might not be granted.

#### Solution

* Verify if the custom role access is provided by checking the groups and role mapping or user and role assignment. You can also open the role under **Admin Settings > Roles**, select the **Assignees** tab, and confirm whether the user appears as a **Direct** assignee or **via** a group. If not, add users to groups that have access or assign roles to the appropriate users.
* Ensure you have granted the necessary access to the datasources used by the application under the _Datasources & Environments_ tab when configuring the custom role. For more details on permissions, see [Permissions](/advanced-concepts/granular-access-control/reference/permissions). For more details on reviewing assignees, see [View role assignees](/advanced-concepts/granular-access-control/roles#view-role-assignees).

## App Viewers can invite other users

A user with the **App Viewer** role can open the **Share** dialog and invite other people to the workspace or app. In the role editor, the App Viewer role shows the **Associate Role** permission.

#### Cause

This is expected. Every default workspace and application role can invite others with the same role or a lower one. An App Viewer can invite others only as App Viewers. A Developer can invite others as Developers or App Viewers. If the invited email address is new to the instance, Appsmith creates an account for it. For details, see [Invite and Share Permissions](/advanced-concepts/granular-access-control/reference/invite-and-share-permissions).

#### Solution

Default roles can't be edited. To give users access without the ability to invite, assign them a custom role and remove their default roles. For steps, see [Restrict Who Can Invite Users](/advanced-concepts/granular-access-control/how-to-guides/restrict-user-invites).

## Unable to invite users

You can open a workspace or app, but the workspace has no **Share** button, or the app's **Share** dialog has no field for inviting users.

#### Cause

Inviting is available only to users who hold a default role on the workspace or app, such as Administrator, Developer, or App Viewer. Users who get access only through custom roles can't invite others.

#### Solution

Ask a workspace Administrator to invite the people you want to add, or ask your instance administrator to assign them a role. For more information, see [Who sees the Share button](/advanced-concepts/granular-access-control/reference/invite-and-share-permissions#who-sees-the-share-button).

## Group is not listed when sharing

When you share a workspace or app, the group you want to invite doesn't appear in the **Share** dialog.

#### Cause

To invite a group, you need **View** permission on that group.

#### Solution

Ask your instance administrator to add **View** on that group to one of your roles, on the **Groups & Roles** tab under **Groups**. For more information, see [Invite a group](/advanced-concepts/granular-access-control/reference/invite-and-share-permissions#invite-a-group).

## Getting help

If you are unable to resolve your error, please reach out to support via the chat widget on the page.
